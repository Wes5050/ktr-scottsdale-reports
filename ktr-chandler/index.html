How this is counted

What's included.

Parties come from Chandler's own Google Ads account (the "KTR Chandler - Party Purchase" conversion): a party counts only when the family clicked a Chandler Google ad before booking online, starting Sep 24, 2026. Online bookings collect a deposit, so each party is valued at $300 for estimated value, return on ad spend and cost per party; "Collected online" is the deposits actually charged. Google splits credit when a family clicked more than one ad, so party counts can include fractions. Calls from ads and store-visit estimates are not included.
